# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and inclusive community focused on practical collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume positive intent
- Be specific and factual when giving feedback
- Keep discussions technical and respectful
- Prioritize reproducibility and safety in security conversations

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Public disclosure of active vulnerabilities without coordinated disclosure
- Sharing private data, credentials, or sensitive operational details

## Enforcement

Project maintainers can remove comments, close threads, or block participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to `conduct@byteworthy.io`.
