# HifzDefend Branding Guidelines

## Brand Identity

### Name
**HifzDefend** (حفظ - Protection/Preservation)

- Pronunciation: "Hifz-Defend"
- Meaning: حفظ (Hifz) = Protection/Preservation in Arabic

### Tagline
"Preserving Your Digital Safety"

## Logo

### Primary Logo
- Shield icon with "HifzDefend" wordmark
- Colors: Blue gradient (#2563EB to #1E40AF)
- Usage: Main branding, website header, app icon

### Icon-Only
- Shield symbol
- Usage: Favicon, app tray icon, shortcuts

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Color** - Primary use
2. **White** - Dark backgrounds
3. **Black** - Light backgrounds
4. **Monochrome** - Print, small sizes

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Blue 600**: #2563EB (Primary brand color)
- **Blue 700**: #1E40AF (Hover states, accents)
- **Blue 900**: #1E3A8A (Dark mode primary)

### Secondary Colors
- **Green 500**: #22C55E (Success, protection active)
- **Red 500**: #EF4444 (Threats, warnings)
- **Yellow 500**: #EAB308 (Warnings, trial status)
- **Gray 500**: #6B7280 (Text, borders)

### Neutral Colors
- **White**: #FFFFFF
- **Gray 50**: #F9FAFB (Backgrounds)
- **Gray 900**: #111827 (Dark mode backgrounds)
- **Gray 950**: #030712 (Deepest dark)

- Hero images: 1920x1080

- Thumbnails: 640x360

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- Professional but approachable
- Technical yet accessible
- Confident and reassuring
- Educational

### Writing Style
- **Active voice**: "HifzDefend protects your system"
- **Clear**: Avoid jargon when possible
- **Concise**: Short sentences, bullet points
- **Consistent**: Use same terminology throughout

### Terminology
- **Scan** (not "check" or "search")
- **Threat** (not "virus" or "malware" generically)
- **Quarantine** (not "isolate" or "remove")
- **Protection** (not "defense" or "security" alone)
- **AI Analysis** (not "machine learning" or "AI detection")

## Accessibility

### Color Contrast
- WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 for normal text
- WCAG AAA preferred: 7:1 for normal text
- Large text (18pt+): 3:1 minimum

### Focus States
- Visible focus ring: 2px Blue 500
- Skip to content link
- Keyboard navigation support

### Alt Text
- All images: Descriptive alt text
- Icons: aria-label when standalone
- Buttons: Clear, action-oriented text

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's ✓
- Use official logo files
- Maintain minimum clearance (1x height)
- Use approved color palette
- Follow spacing guidelines
- Maintain aspect ratio

### Don'ts ✗
- Distort or stretch logo
- Change logo colors arbitrarily
- Add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
- Use low-resolution images
- Combine with competitor logos
